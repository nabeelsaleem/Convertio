Why You Need a Facebook Cover Preview Tool

Facebook covers render wide on desktop and cropped taller on phones, so edge text and logos often get cut off. Upload your image to preview both views with a live safe-area grid — everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Facebook Cover Photo Dimensions

For a standard Facebook Page, the recommended upload is 820 pixels wide by 312 pixels tall — matching the desktop layout. The mobile app uses a taller container (roughly 640 by 360), so one image must satisfy two aspect ratios (about 2.62:1 on desktop versus 16:9 on mobile): build on a slightly taller 820×360 canvas that gives both layouts enough image to crop from without stretching.

How Does Facebook Crop Cover Photo on Mobile vs Desktop?

Facebook fills each container while keeping proportions, then crops the overflow — about 24 pixels top and bottom on desktop, about 90 pixels from each side on mobile. That is why a headline centered on desktop can lose letters on phones; most browsing is mobile, so preview the mobile view before finalizing.

Calculating the Facebook Cover Photo Safe Area

The safe area — about 640 by 312 pixels — is the overlap of the desktop and mobile crops, visible on every device. Put every critical element inside it: headlines, logos, faces, contact details, call-to-action text. The checker's grid overlay makes breaches obvious.

Understanding Facebook Group and Event Banner Dimensions

Groups and events use different sizes: group covers are 1640 by 856 pixels (1.91:1) and scale proportionally on mobile, but on desktop the bottom strip hides behind navigation — keep text out of the lower quarter. Event banners are 1200 by 628 pixels, the feed link-preview ratio.

Technical Resolution, DPI, and Facebook Image Compression

Dimensions are half the battle; the other half is Facebook's compression, which re-encodes uploads and blurs sharp text. Fight it: export graphics-heavy covers as PNG (not JPEG) in sRGB, design at double size (1640×720) for Retina screens, and keep files lean — small, clean files compress best.

Navigating the Profile Picture Overlap Zones

Your cover shares space with the profile picture, page name, and action buttons: the circular photo overlaps the bottom-left on desktop and shifts toward the center on mobile. Keep faces and key words clear of those zones, and use the checker's overlay guides to verify.
