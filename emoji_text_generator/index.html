Best Practices for Social Media

Because each letter is generated using a grid of emojis, different platforms will handle the text differently based on their character limits:

  • Discord & WhatsApp: These platforms have high character limits, making them perfect for full sentences and large community announcements.
  • Twitter / X: With a strict 280-character limit, stick to short, impactful words (3 to 5 letters) like "WOW" or "SALE".
  • High Contrast: For the best visual results, choose foreground and background emojis that contrast heavily (for example, a bright yellow star ⭐ on a dark black square ⬛).
