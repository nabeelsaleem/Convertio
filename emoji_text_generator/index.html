How to Use the Emoji Text Generator

This emoji text generator draws every letter you type as a 5×5 grid of emojis. You choose one emoji for the letter shapes and a different one for the background, and the tool builds a large block-letter version of your text that you can copy and paste anywhere. Because the output is plain Unicode text, it works in almost any app that supports emoji.

Quick Start Guide

  1. Type your message: Enter a word or short phrase (3–12 letters works best) into the input box.
  2. Pick your emojis: Choose a “Text Emoji” to form the letter shapes and a “Bg Emoji” for the background cells.
  3. Copy and paste: Hit the copy button above the preview, then paste your big emoji text into Discord, WhatsApp, Instagram, or anywhere else.

Choosing the Right Emojis

Readability comes down to contrast. The letters are formed by the “text emoji” sitting on a field of the “background emoji,” so the two need to look clearly different at a glance. A bright star ⭐ on a black square ⬛ reads beautifully; two mid-tone faces blur together. Solid shapes like squares, circles, and hearts work best for the background, while anything bold and distinctive — fire 🔥, lightning ⚡, or a contrasting color — makes the letter strokes pop. Switch between the two roles with the buttons under the picker to try combinations quickly; the preview updates live as you type.

Mind the Character Limits

Each letter costs 30 emojis (a 5×5 grid plus spacing), and every emoji can count as two characters on some platforms. Keep that in mind when you pick where to post:

  • Discord: The 2,000-character limit comfortably fits long words and even full sentences — ideal for server announcements and welcome banners.
  • WhatsApp & Telegram: Generous limits make them great for big birthday messages, invitations, and group-chat shout-outs.
  • X (Twitter): With only 280 characters, stick to short punchy words of 3–5 letters like “WOW” or “SALE”.
  • Instagram & TikTok: Bio lines and comment sections accept emoji text well; keep designs short so they render in one clean block.

Creative Ways to Use Emoji Text

Beyond the obvious Discord announcements, big emoji letters are a staple of group chats and fan communities: countdowns (“3…2…1”), event headers (“PARTY”, “GG” after a win), birthday surprises spelled out across a WhatsApp thread, or a signature sign-off for a YouTube comment. Match the emojis to the occasion — pumpkins 🎃 on orange squares for Halloween, hearts ❤️ on pink for Valentine’s — and the design carries the mood by itself. Everything runs in your browser, so you can experiment freely: nothing is uploaded, and there are no watermarks on anything you copy.
