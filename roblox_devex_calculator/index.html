Why the 30% Marketplace Fee Matters

One of the most common mistakes is negotiating payouts against raw sales figures. Roblox takes a 30% marketplace fee on every sale, so 1,000,000 Robux in gross game revenue leaves you with 700,000 earned Robux — worth about $2,660 at the current DevEx rate, not the $3,800 the raw number suggests. Always run partner splits and revenue targets on the net figure.

The “Fees & Prep” tab in the calculator above converts gross sales to net earnings by removing the 30% marketplace fee automatically, so the numbers you bring into the DevEx converter are already accurate.
