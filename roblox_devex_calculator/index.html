How the 2026 Three-Rate DevEx Formula Works

Roblox separates earned Robux by source and earn date, prioritizing the highest rate first when you cash out:

Gross USD Payout = (U.S. 18+ Robux × $0.0054) + ((Standard Robux + Roblox Plus Robux) × $0.0038) + (Legacy Robux × $0.0035)
Roblox Plus Robux = (Affiliates × 250 × Months[1-3]) + (Qualifying Pvt. Server Subs × min(0.70 × Server Price, 100)) + (In-Game Transfers × 0.10)
Net Take-Home Estimate = Gross USD Payout × (1 - Estimated Tax Rate)

Worked Example: Suppose your experience earns 100,000 standard Robux ($380.00) plus 50,000 qualifying U.S. 18+ Robux ($270.00), for a combined gross payout of $650.00 USD (an effective blended rate of $0.00433/R$). If you set aside 20% for taxes ($130.00), your estimated net take-home payout is $520.00 USD.

Roblox Plus Creator Earnings and the 30% Marketplace Fee

Under Roblox Plus, creators can stack three DevEx-eligible bonus streams on top of regular sales: 250 R$/month for a new affiliate subscriber's first 3 consecutive paid months (up to 750 R$ per subscriber), up to 100 R$ per qualifying private-server subscriber based on min(70% × server price, 100) when your server ranks in their top 5 paid servers with 60+ minutes played, and 10% of in-game Robux transfers triggered via the Transfers API.

For standard sales, remember Roblox deducts a 30% marketplace fee before Robux become DevEx-eligible. Use our Roblox Game Pass Calculator to price passes accurately or check engagement payouts with our Roblox Premium Payout Calculator.

Formula and Limitations

  • The $0.0054 U.S. 18+ rate applies strictly to qualifying spend from age-verified U.S. 18+ players in compliant experiences (R15-only or qualifying custom avatars), not to your entire wallet balance.
  • Only earned Robux qualify for DevEx; purchased or user-received transfer Robux cannot be cashed out (though the creator's 10% in-game transfer share is DevEx-eligible).
  • Non-USD currency conversions and tax sliders are planning estimates only and do not constitute tax, legal, or financial advice.
