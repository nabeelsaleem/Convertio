Thumbnail Resolutions, Explained

YouTube keeps several copies of each video's thumbnail at different sizes. Here is what each one means, from sharpest to smallest:

  • Max Resolution (maxresdefault.jpg): 1280 x 720 pixels — the sharpest thumbnail YouTube stores. Always choose this when it is available.
  • Standard Definition (sddefault.jpg): 640 x 480 pixels — a solid middle ground for blog embeds.
  • High Quality (hqdefault.jpg): 480 x 360 pixels — the classic thumbnail size YouTube shows in lists.
  • Medium Quality (mqdefault.jpg): 320 x 180 pixels — fine for small previews and placeholders.
  • Default (default.jpg): 120 x 90 pixels — tiny; only useful as a last resort.

One thing to know: YouTube does not store true 4K thumbnails. Custom thumbnails are capped at 1280 x 720 at upload time, so maxresdefault is the highest quality that exists for any video. Not every video has it, either — older uploads or low-resolution videos may top out at the smaller sizes, and the tool shows you exactly what is available.
