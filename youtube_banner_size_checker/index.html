What Is the Official YouTube Banner Size?

The recommended YouTube banner size is 2560 × 1440 pixels (16:9 aspect ratio) with a maximum file size of 6 MB. The minimum upload size YouTube accepts is 2048 × 1152 pixels. To make sure your channel name and logo never get cut off on phones, keep all key text inside the central 1546 × 423 pixel safe area.

YouTube Banner Display Sizes by Device (TV vs. Desktop vs. Mobile)

Because YouTube serves one 2560 × 1440 image to every viewer, each device crops the edges differently. A design that looks spacious on a smart TV loses its top, bottom, and side margins on a smartphone:

Device Visible Banner Area What Gets Cropped
TV (Smart TV / Console) 2560 × 1440 px Nothing — the full 16:9 canvas is visible.
Desktop (Max Width) 2560 × 423 px Top 508 px and bottom 509 px are cropped off.
Tablet 1855 × 423 px Top/bottom plus 352 px on each horizontal side are cropped.
Mobile (All Devices Safe Zone) 1546 × 423 px Top/bottom plus 507 px on both left and right edges are cropped.

Pro Tips to Prevent Cropping & Blurry Channel Art

  • Keep key branding centered: Place your channel name, upload schedule, and logo inside the central 1546 × 423 safe box. Use the outer areas for clean background textures or subtle visuals.
  • Avoid the bottom-right corner on desktop: YouTube places clickable channel links in the bottom-right corner of the desktop banner strip, which can cover text placed too close to that edge.
  • Stay under 6 MB without crushing quality: If a detailed PNG exceeds 6 MB, switch the export format above to JPG (90–92% quality) or compress further with our image size reducer.
